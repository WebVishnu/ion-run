How to play
  • Change lane with the arrow keys, A and D, or swipe. The left lane has oncoming traffic and pays double.
  • Pass cars closely to build a streak. Faster and closer scores more.
  • Drive over green charge pads. When the battery hits zero, the run ends.
  • Space fires the thruster hop. In the air, left and right roll, up and down flip. Land level for a bonus.
  • In a tunnel, hold Shift (or hold the Hop button) to stick to the ceiling and drive upside down.

Ion Run is a fictional game. The car is an original design and is not affiliated with any vehicle manufacturer.
